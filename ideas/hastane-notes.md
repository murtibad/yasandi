# Devlet hastanesi notes (from a YouTube AI summary, 1 Oct 2026)

Patterns only, retold. Doctors and nurses are tired people inside a tired system, never villains. The joke is the system and the waiting room.

## Structure: the işe giriş raporu

You start a new job tomorrow and need a health report today. The hospital closes at 16:00. The report needs stamps from several departments (göz, KBB, akciğer filmi, kan tahlili) and a final signature. Each department is a step with its own small disaster. Every step can eat time; the clock is the pressure ("Saat 15:12. İki imza eksik.").

## Moments to use

- **Waiting room competition:** amcalar compare illnesses ("O da bir şey mi, benim taşım ceviz kadar!"). The player can join; exaggerating wins respect, or a crowd, or a seat.
- **The queue screen:** your number blinks and disappears; the speaker calls a number that does not exist; "A127" stays on screen for forty minutes.
- **Danışma:** "Sistem kapalı" / "Sistem güncelleniyor" / "Yarın sabah erken gel."
- **"Selamı var":** someone walks in without knocking with greetings from somebody's uncle and is seen before you.
- **Dr. Google:** player says "internetten baktım, bende nadir bir hastalık var". It must get a reply.
- **Lost blood tube:** the lab says it never arrived; the nurse says she sent it.
- **Pharmacy:** "Bu ilaç yok, karşıdakine sor." The one across says the same.
- **Selling your queue number** to the teyze who has been there since 7.
- **Kantin:** a tost and a tea cost more than the examination.
- Small details: MHRS, e-Nabız, everyone calls the doctor "hocam", refakatçi sandalyesi, "tahliller çıkınca gel", the doctor reading results while holding the phone with his shoulder.

## Not used

- The "deli raporu" bit (jokes on mental illness) and the sperm-test room (off tone for a game shared in family groups).
