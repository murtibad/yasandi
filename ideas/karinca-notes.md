## Setting and stake

A Sunday family picnic. From the ground: mangal smoke, a semaver, karpuz rinds, a fallen simit crumb, a tablecloth with folds like mountain ranges. Stake in the first lines: the colony is hungry, and the queen expects you back before dark with something better than dust.

## Facts that are funny on their own

- **Ant mill:** ants follow their own scent trail and walk in a circle forever. A player who insists on "izi takip ediyorum" starts circling. Good ending.
- **Scale:** carries 50 times its weight. A sugar cube is Everest, a tea glass a glass tower, a terlik the end of the world, a broom a tornado.
- **Head as a door:** some soldier ants block the nest entrance with their heads.
- **Antenna greeting:** ants touch antennae to share news. Two ants bump into each other like neighbors on the street.

## Moves and reactions worth keeping

- Planting a flag on a sugar cube ("burası bizim"): a giant hand drops it into a tea glass, or someone eats it.
- Playing dead: carried to the garbage, a free ride.
- Presenting a speck of dust to the queen: she twitches her antennae in contempt.
- Stopping another ant for a chat: the scent traffic jams, the ones behind start honking with their antennae.
- Trying to ride a beetle: "ben taksi değilim".

## Not used
