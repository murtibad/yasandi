# Keko notes (from a YouTube AI summary, 1 Oct 2026)

Patterns only. Never copy the video's lines or its characters (no "Kuşçu Feyzo", no "mitoz bölünme" as a named bit). Keep it a 30-second street moment, not a crime story: no robberies, no gangs, no police raids.

## Use in `goz-temasi`

- **The whistle.** Player talks back; keko whistles once, and from every corner someone appears: from the bakkal, from behind a parked car, one from a balcony in slippers. Good as a consequence of a cocky answer, and a funny ending if the player whistles back and nobody comes for him.
- **The abi ends it.** An older abi walks by: "— Bizim çocuktur, boş ver." The fight evaporates in one sentence. The keko suddenly very polite.
- **Details for `look` and narration:** squatting on the curb, kehribar tespih, a big ring with a crown on it, heavy spicy perfume you smell before you see him, a bag of çekirdek, phone always in hand.
- **The late-night story.** Player types "instagramına bakıyorum" / "story'ne bakıyorum" / "seni takip ediyorum": the latest story is a sad quote about loyalty with a crying emoji and a lion.
- **Mother.** Fear and respect: one call from his mother ends any standoff ("— Efendim anne? Yok anne, bir şey yapmıyorum.").

## Not used

- Crime plots (robbery, bird traded for a car), the video's named characters, the AI's suggested reply lines (too generic; our own lines are better).
