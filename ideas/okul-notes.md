# Classroom notes (from a YouTube AI summary, 1 Oct 2026)

Patterns only, retold. Teachers are people whose patience is being tested, never villains. Warm, nostalgic, everyone has sat in this room.

## Stake

The teacher says "Kağıtları çıkarın" (surprise quiz) or opens the sınıf defteri to pick someone for the board, and you did not do the homework. The person you like sits two rows ahead and will see everything (rol pası: they are the one the player fills in with `acceptAny`).

## Moments to use

- Yoklama lie for a friend: "— Hocam az önce buradaydı, tuvalete gitti." It comes back to bite you.
- "— Hocam su içmeye gidebilir miyim?" "— Gelince tahtaya kalkıyorsun."
- A paper plane from the back row hits your head; the teacher turns and looks at you.
- "Kim konuştu?" while the whole class was talking.
- The bell rings in the middle of the teacher's sentence; the half sentence hangs in the air.
- English class: "Only English!" and the class instantly argues in Turkish.
- The teacher's story from "bizim zamanımızda", the retiring teacher who gave up years ago, the young one who thinks this topic will change your life.
- Student types: arka sıra, sınıf başkanı who reports everything, the one who reminds the teacher about homework, the one who asks to go to the toilet every lesson.
- Small details: tebeşir, sınıf defteri, "velinle görüşeceğim", nöbetçi öğretmen, "bu sınıf beni çok yordu", "teneffüse daha var".

## Not used

- A teacher insulting students ("geri zekalı"), and history jokes built on real historical figures from the video.
